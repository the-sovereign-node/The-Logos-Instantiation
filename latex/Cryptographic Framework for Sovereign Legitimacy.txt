\begin{abstract}
We construct, from first principles in set theory, type theory, and cryptography, a fully deterministic decision procedure for the legality or illegality of any governmental act, regime, or claim to sovereignty. Starting from primitive notions of objects, signatures, and delegation relations, we define a typed universe of Legal Acts as dependently typed records. We equip this universe with guarded update semantics whose preconditions enforce competence (\(nemo\ dat\ quod\ non\ habet\)) and whose postconditions preserve a global validity invariant.  

A dual-layer architecture separates immutable Legislative competence (Extended Key Usage bitmasks) from context-sensitive Judicial interpretation (Judicial Hash \(\mathcal{J}\)). Epochal evolution is mediated by Epoch Transition Certificates (ETCs) protected by Duress Sensors. Physical inputs are internalized via Byzantine Fault Tolerant observation sets equipped with fraud proofs and economic slashing.  

The resulting Sovereign Kernel computes a unique output in \(\{\text{VALID},\ \text{VOID}:\text{FAULT TYPE}\}\) for any queried act or regime once the input DAG, bitmask assignments, and oracle-validated facts are supplied. All computation is recursive, acyclic, and machine-checkable. Subjectivity is confined to auditable input encoding; the decision procedure itself contains none.  

This paper supplies the complete blueprint: definitions, axioms, lemmas, theorems with proofs, algorithmic specifications, and hybrid dynamical coupling of probabilistic genesis to deterministic maintenance. The framework decides legality without reference to political preference, judicial discretion, or natural-language ambiguity.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: The Epistemological Imperative for a Computable Validity Predicate}

From first principles, any claim that a regime or act possesses authority must ultimately rest on a justification chain. In classical jurisprudence this chain terminates (or fails to terminate) in Kelsen's \emph{Grundnorm}---a presupposed basic norm whose validity is anchored in factual effectiveness. The regress is logical: a constitution is valid because enacted under a prior norm; that norm under an earlier one; and so on.  

We reject any non-computable termination. Instead, we demand a \emph{decision procedure} \(\mathcal{D}\) that, given a finite, explicitly presented input (a claimed DAG of acts together with cryptographic evidence and BFT-validated observations), returns in finite time one of a finite set of verdicts:
\begin{equation*}
\begin{split}
\mathcal{D}(\text{input}) \in \{&\text{VALID},\ \text{VOID}:\text{PRIVILEGE\_ESCALATION\_FAULT}, \\
&\text{VOID}:\text{DEADLOCK},\ \text{VOID}:\text{DURESS}, \\
&\text{VOID}:\text{JUS\_COGENS\_VIOLATION},\ \dots \}.
\end{split}
\end{equation*}
The procedure must be purely syntactic once the input is fixed, independent of the semantic content of payloads beyond their typed structure, and invariant-preserving under every legal mutation of the state.  

This requirement forces us to begin with the most primitive objects: sets, functions, relations, cryptographic primitives (assumed to satisfy standard security properties: collision resistance of hash, unforgeability of signatures under chosen-message attack), and typed records. All higher structure---sovereignty, legitimacy, legality---is constructed by successive definition, never by appeal to intuition or political practice. The resulting system constitutes an absolute blueprint: feed it the requisite evidence and it computes legality or illegality without remainder.

\section{Axiomatic Foundations}

We begin with the most elementary notions required for a formal theory of authority.

\begin{definition}[Cryptographic Primitive]
Let \(\mathcal{H}\) be a collision-resistant hash function and \(\Sigma = (\text{KeyGen},\ \text{Sign},\ \text{Verify})\) a signature scheme that is existentially unforgeable under chosen-message attack. A \emph{signature} on a message \(m\) under private key \(sk\) is \(\sigma = \text{Sign}(sk, \mathcal{H}(m))\). Verification succeeds if and only if \(\text{Verify}(pk, \mathcal{H}(m), \sigma) = 1\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Delegation Relation]
A \emph{delegation} is a triple \((p, c, \sigma)\) where \(p\) is a parent object, \(c\) a child object, and \(\sigma\) a valid signature on the tuple \((c.\text{id}, c.\text{eku}, c.\text{nc}, \dots)\) under the public key associated with \(p\).
\end{definition}

We now construct the state-space of factual criteria.

\begin{definition}[State-Space Vector]
Let the factual criteria of any regime be represented by a vector
\[
C = (c_1, c_2, c_3, c_4, c_5) \in [0,1]^5,
\]
where each coordinate is a normalized real number obtained from an auditable observation process (formalized later as BFT-validated Proofs of Observation). The coordinates are interpreted as:
\begin{itemize}
\item \(c_1 = P\): Demographic Permanence (measure of stable population under effective administration),
\item \(c_2 = T\): Territorial Integrity (normalized measure of de-facto control over claimed topological boundaries),
\item \(c_3 = E\): Effective Monopolization of Force (Weberian capacity to enforce order),
\item \(c_4 = R\): Diplomatic Recognition (weighted aggregate of peer recognition and treaty relations),
\item \(c_5 = J\): Jus Cogens Adherence (multiplicative filter for peremptory-norm compliance).
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{axiom}[Non-Compensatory Character of Statehood]
Statehood is non-compensatory: the absence of any foundational element cannot be offset by surplus in another. Consequently any legitimate aggregation function \(L\) must satisfy \(L(C) = 0\) whenever \(c_i = 0\) for some \(i \in \{1,2,3,4\}\) or when the normative filter \(c_5 = 0\).
\end{axiom}

\begin{definition}[Legitimacy Production Function]
The factual legitimacy index is given by the multiplicative (Cobb-Douglas-style) function
\[
L(G) = c_5 \prod_{i=1}^{4} c_i^{w_i},
\]
where the elasticity weights satisfy \(\sum_{i=1}^{4} w_i = 1\) and \(w_i > 0\). The weights are treated as parameters of the jurisdictional model and may be calibrated subject to the constraint that the function respects Axiom 1.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Zero-Set Characterization]
\(L(G) = 0\) if and only if there exists \(k \in \{1,2,3,4\}\) such that \(c_k = 0\), or \(c_5 = 0\).

\begin{proof}
(\(\Rightarrow\)) Suppose \(L(G) = 0\). If \(c_5 = 0\) we are done. Otherwise the finite product must vanish, hence at least one base \(c_k = 0\) (\(k \le 4\)).  
(\(\Leftarrow\)) Immediate: any zero factor forces the whole expression to zero.  
Thus the lemma holds.
\end{proof}
\end{lemma}

The index \(L(G)\) quantifies \emph{factual} effectiveness. It does not yet decide \emph{legal} competence. For that we require a dynamical model of genesis and a typed model of authority transmission.

\subsection{The Markovian Model of Singular Genesis}

Political reality prior to institutionalization may be idealized as a stochastic process on a finite state space.

\begin{definition}[Regime-Type State Space]
Let \(S = \{s_1, s_2, s_3, s_4\}\) where
\begin{itemize}
\item \(s_1\): Hyper-fragmented Anarchy,
\item \(s_2\): Insurgent Competition for Monopoly of Force,
\item \(s_3\): De-Facto Consolidation,
\item \(s_4\): De-Jure Institutionalization.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Discrete-Time Markov Chain for Political Evolution]
Let \(X_t\) be the regime type at discrete epoch \(t\), with row-stochastic transition matrix \(P = (p_{ij})\) where \(p_{ij} = \Pr(X_{t+1}=s_j \mid X_t = s_i)\). The chain is assumed irreducible and aperiodic on its recurrent class (standard ergodicity conditions guaranteeing a unique stationary distribution).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Existence and Uniqueness of Stationary Distribution]
Under the irreducibility and aperiodicity assumptions there exists a unique probability vector \(\pi = (\pi_1, \pi_2, \pi_3, \pi_4)\) satisfying
\[
\pi = \pi P, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^4 \pi_i = 1, \quad \pi_i \ge 0.
\]
The vector \(\pi\) is the unique solution of the linear system \((I - P^\top)\pi^\top = 0\) augmented by the normalization constraint.
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}[Numerical Grundnorm]
The \emph{Algorithmic Grundnorm} of a successful organic consolidation is the long-run proportion of time spent in the institutionalized state:
\[
l(S_0) := \pi_4.
\]
When \(\pi_4 \to 1\) the singularity furnishes a self-signed root LegalAct with initial legitimacy index normalized to 1 (subject to cryptographic self-signature validity).
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
The DTMC furnishes only the \emph{probabilistic genesis} of a root. All subsequent evolution is deterministic and governed by the guarded typed semantics introduced in the next part of the paper. The hybrid coupling (probabilistic root + deterministic maintenance) is derived in full in Part 3.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Auditable Status of the Transition Matrix]
For any concrete invocation of the decision procedure \(\mathcal{D}\) (or its Validate realization) on a historical or prospective regime, the specific transition matrix \(P\) (or a compact representation thereof, e.g., a finite set of matrices equipped with probability weights or uncertainty bounds) is supplied explicitly as part of the auditable Inputs. If historical transition data exist, they are encoded as BFT-validated Proofs of Observation and are themselves subject to fraud-proof challenge inside the kernel. Consequently, the choice or estimation of \(P\) carries no residual subjectivity external to the formal system; it is an explicit, challengeable component of the input artifact whose consistency with observed history can be litigated by the same guarded-update and Validate machinery.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Continuity and Robustness of the Numerical Grundnorm]
Let \(\mathcal{P}\) be the set of row-stochastic \(4\times 4\) matrices that are irreducible and aperiodic. Equip \(\mathcal{P}\) with the total-variation metric
\[
d_{\mathrm{TV}}(P,Q) = \frac12\sum_{i,j=1}^4 |p_{ij}-q_{ij}|.
\]
The map \(P\mapsto\pi_4(P)\) (the fourth coordinate of the unique stationary distribution) is continuous on \(\mathcal{P}\). Moreover, if the family satisfies a uniform Doeblin minorization condition (there exist \(\delta>0\) and a probability measure \(\nu\) on \(S\) such that \(p_{ij}\ge\delta\nu_j\) for all \(i,j\)), then the map is Lipschitz continuous with respect to \(d_{\mathrm{TV}}\), with Lipschitz constant depending only on \(\delta\) and the state-space cardinality.

\begin{proof}
The stationary distribution satisfies the linear system \(\pi(I-P)=0\) together with the normalization \(\sum\pi_i=1\). On the open set of irreducible aperiodic matrices the fundamental matrix (or group inverse) of \(I-P\) exists and varies continuously (in fact analytically) with the entries of \(P\). The coordinate functional extracting \(\pi_4\) is linear and therefore continuous. Under the uniform Doeblin condition the chain is geometrically ergodic with rate uniform in a neighborhood of any \(P\in\mathcal{P}\); standard perturbation theory for Markov operators then yields Lipschitz continuity of the stationary map on that neighborhood. Consequently, any uncertainty interval or probability distribution over admissible matrices \(P\) propagates into a rigorously bounded interval (or semiring-valued distribution) for the root legitimacy threshold \(l(S_0)\), which may be encoded directly in the input to Validate or in the semiring-valued legitimacy extension.
\end{proof}
\end{lemma}

\section{The Sovereign Kernel: Typed Universe, Dual Layers, and Guarded Semantics}

Having constructed the factual index \(L(G)\) and the probabilistic genesis of a root singularity via the DTMC, we now lift these notions into a fully typed, cryptographically signed, and mutation-guarded structure. All subsequent development proceeds from first principles: we define objects, signatures, and delegation relations as primitive predicates, then build higher-order structure by successive inductive definition. The resulting Sovereign Kernel supplies the absolute, subjectivity-free blueprint for deciding the legality or illegality of any act or regime.

\subsection{Primitive Building Blocks}

\begin{definition}[Signature Verification Predicate]
Let \(\text{VerifySig}(pk, m, \sigma)\) be the predicate that holds if and only if the signature \(\sigma\) on message \(m\) (typically \(\mathcal{H}(m)\)) is valid under public key \(pk\) according to the unforgeable signature scheme \(\Sigma\). This is a decidable, purely syntactic predicate.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Delegation Relation]
A delegation from parent object \(p\) to child object \(c\) is witnessed by a signature \(\sigma\) such that \(\text{VerifySig}(p.\text{pk}, \langle c.\text{id}, c.\text{eku}, c.\text{nc}, \dots \rangle, \sigma)\) holds. The relation is asymmetric and acyclic by construction of the DAG.
\end{definition}

\subsection{The Typed Universe of Legal Acts}

We define Legal Acts as dependently typed records. In a dependently typed setting this ensures that every field is well-formed with respect to its context (e.g., EKU bits are elements of a finite permission set).

\begin{definition}[LegalAct Record Type]
A \emph{LegalAct} is an element of the record type:
\begin{align*}
\text{LegalAct} := \{ & \text{id} : \text{ID}, && (\text{unique identifier}), \\
  & \text{issuer} : \text{Option}(\text{LegalAct}), \\
  & \text{pk} : \text{PublicKey}, \\
  & \text{eku} : \text{EKU}, && (\text{bitmask } \in \mathbb{B}^m), \\
  & \text{nc} : \text{NameConstraint}, && (\text{topological region}), \\
  & \text{payload} : \text{Payload}, && (\text{typed content}), \\
  & \text{t}_{\text{start}}, \text{t}_{\text{end}} : \text{Time}, \\
  & \text{signature} : \text{Signature} \}
\end{align*}
where \(\text{EKU}\) is a finite set of permission bits (including at minimum \(E_{\text{admin}}\), \(E_{\text{dispose}}\), \(E_{\text{governance}}\), \(E_{\text{issue}}\), \(E_{\text{waive}}\), and reserved jus-cogens positions), and \(\text{NameConstraint}\) is a compact representation of a spatial or jurisdictional region.
\end{definition}

A root singularity is the special case \(\text{issuer} = \text{None}\) together with a self-signature that verifies under its own public key.

\begin{definition}[SovereignNode]
A \emph{SovereignNode} extends a LegalAct with a current legitimacy index and Markov state reference:
\[
\text{SovereignNode} \;:=\; \text{LegalAct} \times [0,1] \times S.
\]
\end{definition}

The entire history of a regime is a finite Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) \(\mathcal{D}\) whose nodes are LegalActs and whose edges are witnessed delegations.

\subsection{Dual-Layer Architecture of the Sovereign Kernel}

To reconcile immutability of core rules with adaptability of interpretation we employ a strict dual-layer isomorphism.

\begin{definition}[Legislative Layer -- Competence Gate]
The Legislative Layer is defined by Extended Key Usage bitmasks and the competence gate function
\[
\Omega(p, h) = 
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{if } (\text{eku}_h\ \&\ \lnot\text{eku}_p) = 0\ (i.e.,\ \text{eku}_h\ \preceq\ \text{eku}_p), \\
0 & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
This directly encodes the maxim \(nemo\ dat\ quod\ non\ habet\): a parent may issue a child only if every permission bit requested by the child is already present in the parent.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Interpretive Layer -- Judicial Hash]
The Interpretive Layer augments every node with a Judicial Hash \(\mathcal{J}_h\) that is a cryptographic commitment (hash) to a node in a separate jurisprudence sub-DAG. When semantic resolution is required (e.g., the meaning of ``reasonable'' in a tax statute), the system follows \(\mathcal{J}_h\) to obtain a signed, precedent-based interpretation without mutating the immutable Legislative bits.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Closure of the Jurisprudence Sub-DAG]
The jurisprudence sub-DAG \(\mathcal{J}\) pointed to by any Judicial Hash \(\mathcal{J}_h\) is itself a finite DAG whose nodes are LegalActs (precedent or interpretive records) and whose edges are witnessed delegations. The structure, mutations, competence gates, Name Constraints, signature verification, guarded-update semantics, and Validate procedure that govern \(\mathcal{J}\) are identical to those that govern the primary sovereignty DAG. Consequently:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Every interpretive resolution is itself a LegalAct whose legitimacy \(L_{\mathcal{J}}(\cdot)\) is computed by the same recursive definition used for the primary DAG.

\item Any dispute concerning the correctness, applicability, or consistency of a precedent is resolved by traversing the delegation path inside \(\mathcal{J}\) and invoking the (identical) Validate procedure on the relevant interpretive act.

\item No infinite regress arises: interpretive authority is always grounded in a root within \(\mathcal{J}\), which is either self-signed (under the same Markovian genesis criteria) or linked by a valid Epoch Transition Certificate from a prior interpretive epoch. The dual-layer architecture therefore remains entirely internal to the typed, guarded, machine-checkable universe.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Node Tuple with Dual Layers]
Every LegalAct is realized as the augmented tuple
\[
h = \langle \text{id}_h,\ \text{issuerID},\ \text{eku}_h,\ \mathcal{J}_h,\ \text{nc}_h,\ \text{payload},\ t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}},\ \sigma_h \rangle.
\]
\end{definition}

\subsection{Guarded Update Semantics}

Evolution of the legal DAG occurs exclusively via guarded partial functions. We adopt Hoare-logic notation for precision and machine-checkability.

\begin{definition}[Guarded Update Operation]
The partial function
\[
\text{update}(\mathcal{D},\ \text{signer},\ \text{new\_payload},\ \text{new\_eku},\ \text{new\_nc},\ t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}) :\ \text{Option}(\mathcal{D}')
\]
is defined if and only if the precondition \(\text{Pre}\) holds:
\begin{align*}
\text{Pre} \;:=\; &
L_{\mathcal{D}}(\text{signer}) = 1 \\
&\land\ E_{\text{issue}} \in \text{eku}_{\text{signer}} \\
&\land\ \text{new\_eku} \preceq \text{eku}_{\text{signer}}\quad(\Omega(\text{signer}, h_{\text{new}}) = 1) \\
&\land\ \text{new\_nc} \subseteq \text{nc}_{\text{signer}} \\
&\land\ t_{\text{current}} \in [t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}] \\
&\land\ \text{new\_payload}\ \text{is well-formed w.r.t.\ the payload type system} \\
&\land\ \text{VerifySig}(\text{signer}.\text{pk},\ \mathcal{H}(\text{new\_payload} \Vert \dots),\ \sigma_{\text{new}}).
\end{align*}
If \(\text{Pre}\) holds, the operation returns \(\mathcal{D}' = \mathcal{D} \cup \{h_{\text{new}}\}\) where \(h_{\text{new}}\) is the freshly constructed child with issuer \(\text{signer}\) and the supplied fields.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Legality Preservation -- Invariant Maintenance]
If \(\text{Pre}\) holds for an update on a valid DAG \(\mathcal{D}\) (i.e., \(\forall h \in \mathcal{D},\ L_{\mathcal{D}}(h) = 1\)), then the resulting DAG \(\mathcal{D}'\) remains valid: \(\forall h \in \mathcal{D}',\ L_{\mathcal{D}'}(h) = 1\), \(\mathcal{D}'\) is acyclic, and no privilege-escalation fault is introduced.

\begin{proof}
We proceed by structural induction on the length of the unique path from any node in \(\mathcal{D}'\) to its root in the (extended) DAG.  

\textbf{Base case.} The newly created leaf \(h_{\text{new}}\) has parent \(\text{signer}\) satisfying \(L_{\mathcal{D}}(\text{signer}) = 1\) and \(\Omega(\text{signer}, h_{\text{new}}) = 1\) by \(\text{Pre}\). The recursive definition of legitimacy therefore evaluates to 1 for \(h_{\text{new}}\). Acyclicity holds because we attach a fresh node only to an existing node.  

\textbf{Inductive step.} Consider any descendant of \(h_{\text{new}}\) (or any unchanged node). By the inductive hypothesis on the original valid DAG and the fact that the only structural change is the addition of a single valid child whose parent chain already satisfies the invariant, the property propagates to the entire new DAG. No new privilege-escalation can arise because \(\Omega\) is checked syntactically in \(\text{Pre}\).  

Hence the theorem holds by induction.
\end{proof}
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Cascading Validity]
If a parent \(p\) satisfies \(L_{\mathcal{D}}(p) = 0\), then every descendant \(h\) (i.e., every node reachable from \(p\) by a delegation path) also satisfies \(L_{\mathcal{D}}(h) = 0\). This follows immediately by substitution into the recursive legitimacy definition and is the formal basis of systemic invalidation cascades.

\begin{proof}
Direct from the recursive clause \(L(h) = \dots \cdot L(p)\). If the parent factor is zero, the product is zero regardless of other factors.
\end{proof}
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Category-Theoretic Formulation}

For compositionality and machine verification we lift the construction into category theory.

\begin{definition}[Category \(\mathbf{Leg}\)]
The category \(\mathbf{Leg}\) has:

- Objects: LegalActs (including roots).

- Morphisms: Valid delegation paths (finite sequences of signed parent--child edges satisfying all EKU and NC constraints along the path).

- Composition: Path concatenation.

- Identities: Trivial self-paths at each root.

A waiver that resolves a dual-parent deadlock is a 2-cell filling the square formed by two parallel arrows from conflicting parents to a contested child, thereby equalizing the paths and restoring commutativity.
\end{definition}

The guarded update of Definition 3.4 is a partial functor \(\mathbf{Leg} \rightharpoonup \mathbf{Leg}\) that, when defined, strictly enlarges the diagram while preserving all commuting triangles that encode legitimacy.

\subsection{The Objective Decision Procedure}

We now define the core blueprint function that decides legality or illegality without any subjective element once inputs are fixed.

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Validate(h, \protect$\mathcal{D}$\protect, Inputs)}, label={alg:validate}]
Input: Claimed act h, current DAG D, auditable inputs (EKU/NC assignments, 
       BFT oracle observations with fraud-proof trail)
Output: Verdict in {VALID, VOID:FAULT TYPE} with diagnostic trace

If h not in D:
    Return VOID: NOT_IN_DAG

Reconstruct unique path P = h <- p1 <- ... <- root in D
For each edge (child, parent) along P:
    If not VerifySig(parent.pk, child.tuple, child.signature):
        Return VOID: INVALID_SIGNATURE (at child)
    If not time_valid(child):
        Return VOID: EXPIRED
    If child on CRL:
        Return VOID: REVOKED
    If Omega(parent, child) == 0:
        Return VOID: PRIVILEGE_ESCALATION_FAULT (at child)
    If NameConstraint violation:
        Return VOID: NAME_CONSTRAINT_VIOLATION

If root legitimacy L(root) < theta:
    Return VOID: INSUFFICIENT_GENESIS_MASS
If Judicial Hash resolution fails consistency check:
    Return VOID: INTERPRETIVE_INCONSISTENCY

Return VALID with full verification trace
\end{lstlisting}

The algorithm is purely recursive (or iterative via topological sort) and terminates because the DAG is finite and acyclic. All checks are decidable syntactic predicates once the input artifacts are supplied. The BFT oracle layer (formalized in Part 3) ensures that ``Inputs'' themselves carry cryptographic proof of consensus and are challengeable by fraud proofs.

\begin{remark}[Subjectivity Confinement]
The only non-deterministic or interpretive step is the initial encoding of historical or physical facts into EKU bitmasks, Name Constraints, and oracle observations. These encodings are themselves LegalActs subject to the same guarded update rules and fraud-proof challenges. Once encoded and BFT-validated, the decision procedure \(\text{Validate}\) is fully deterministic, compositional, and free of further subjectivity. The blueprint therefore decides legality/illegality by computation alone.
\end{remark}

\section{Epochal Evolution, Duress Protection, Systemic Resilience, and Hybrid Dynamics}

Having equipped the Sovereign Kernel with typed LegalActs, dual layers, and guarded updates that preserve the global validity invariant, we now address the dynamics of constitutional change and catastrophic failure. All constructions remain strictly first-principles: we extend the existing record type and guarded partial functions with new fields and decidable preconditions; we prove invariance by the same structural induction already employed; and we embed every mechanism inside the objective Validate procedure so that legality/illegality continues to be decided by finite, syntactic computation alone.

\subsection{Epoch Transition Certificates as Guarded Acts}

Constitutional evolution is not an extra-legal political event; it is a special case of guarded update.

\begin{definition}[Epoch]
An \emph{Epoch} \(E_k\) is a versioned state of the Sovereign Protocol consisting of the current DAG of LegalActs, the active EKU bitmask of the Sovereign Governance Committee (SGC), the jurisprudence sub-DAG pointed to by Judicial Hashes, and all pending obligations (treaties, debts, CRL entries).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Epoch Transition Certificate (ETC)]
An ETC is a LegalAct \(h_{\text{ETC}}\) whose payload contains:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A cryptographic hash of the entire prior epoch state \(E_k\) (including all pending obligations),

\item Threshold signatures from an \(m\)-of-\(n\) quorum of the current SGC,

\item Explicit transfer of the \(E_{\text{governance}}\) bitmask to the successor SGC of \(E_{k+1}\),

\item A fresh root or continuation reference for the new epoch.
\end{enumerate}
An ETC is issued only when the following strengthened precondition holds (in addition to the general Pre of Part 2):
\begin{align*}
\text{Pre}_{\text{ETC}} \;:=\; &
\text{Pre (general guarded update)} \\
&\land\ m\text{-of-}n\ \text{threshold signatures valid} \\
&\land\ \text{IntegrityHash}(E_k)\ \text{matches payload} \\

\section{Worked Hybrid Dynamics Example}
Between updates the ODE \(\dot{L} = \alpha (c_3(t) - L)\) has the explicit solution
\[
L(t) = L(t_0) e^{-\alpha (t - t_0)} + \alpha \int_{t_0}^t e^{-\alpha (t - s)} c_3(s) \, ds.
\]
At a valid update the process experiences a multiplicative jump given by the new node’s contribution to the recursive legitimacy definition. Taking expectations over the (guarded-update-governed) jump times and invoking the ergodic theorem for the underlying DTMC yields the long-run formula stated in Part 3.
